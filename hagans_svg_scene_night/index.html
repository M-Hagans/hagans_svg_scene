Night Mountains

I drew a nighttime mountain scene. I used a rect for the sky and ground, a circle and ellipse to make a crescent moon, polygons for the mountains, and a line to make a small star. I grouped the mountains together to give them all a white outline.

Reflection: The hardest part was lining up the ellipse with the circle to make the moon look right. I had to move it a few times before it looked like a crescent. I first used brighter colors for the mountains, but they did not match the night sky. I have seen SVG graphics used on weather apps and websites before.
