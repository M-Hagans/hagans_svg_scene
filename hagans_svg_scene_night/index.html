Night Mountains

I drew the nighttime mountain scene. I used a rect for the sky and ground, a circle and ellipse to make a crescent moon, polygons for the mountains, and a line to make a small star. I grouped the mountains together to give them all a white outline.

Reflection: The hardest shape to position was the ellipse because I had to overlap it perfectly with the circle to make the moon look like a crescent. At first, I tried making the mountains bright purple, but they looked too fake against the dark sky, so I switched to navy blue. I have seen this kind of SVG work on weather websites where a moon or sun changes shape based on the time of day.
